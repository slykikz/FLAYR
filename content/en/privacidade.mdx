## AI assistant and contact form

The assistant is optional and can make mistakes. Messages are sent to OpenAI for response generation, with `store: false`; this setting does not by itself remove all provider security retention. Do not share personal data, passwords or card information. Basic email and long-number redaction is applied but cannot guarantee removal of all personal data. Conversations remain in page memory and are cleared on reload. Only an order status associated with the checkout browser can be supplied to the assistant; names, addresses and payment details are not included in its order context. The assistant cannot modify orders or make refunds. Contact the team for human assistance.

Contact submissions are encrypted in the database and sent through Resend to the private support inbox. Your address is used as the reply-to address. [Confirm the retention period and operating process before launch.]
