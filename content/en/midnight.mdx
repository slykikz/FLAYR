# Midnight 01. Own the night.

The sound of the ball on the floor. The last light on. One more attempt.

Midnight takes its inspiration from a court after hours. Deep black, electric yellow and the energy that remains when everything around you slows down.

## Colour as movement

Contrast connects the silhouette to its surroundings. The design concept combines a strong visual construction with details revealed by light.

## From concept to court

This campaign is a creative exploration by FLAYR. The image was generated with artificial intelligence; the 3D model in the demonstration is a concept, not a technical reproduction of a finished product.

Commercial development requires prototypes and testing. Until then, explore the collection's visual language and find your next movement.
