## Withdrawing from a purchase

You may notify withdrawal within 14 days, calculated according to law from receipt by you or a nominated third party other than the carrier. For one order delivered in separate parts, the period runs from receipt of the last relevant item. Send an unequivocal decision to [business email/address] before the deadline, or use the [online form](/legal/resolucao). The model form below is optional. No account or prior authorisation is required to submit notice.

Return or hand over the goods to [recipient/business returns address] within 14 days after giving notice. [Choose the actual policy: the customer pays the previously disclosed direct return cost / FLAYR provides free returns.] For goods that cannot normally be returned by post, disclose the cost or legally appropriate estimate before purchase. You may examine goods as necessary to establish their nature, characteristics and functioning; excessive handling may result in liability for diminished value as provided by law.

We reimburse covered payments, including standard outbound delivery, within 14 days of notification. An additional charge for a more expensive delivery option chosen by you is not refunded where the law permits. We may withhold reimbursement until receiving the goods or evidence of return, whichever occurs first, unless we offer to collect them. Reimbursement uses the original payment method unless expressly agreed otherwise without fees to you. See [Decree-Law 24/2014](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/24-2014-572450).

## Exceptions and faulty goods

Exceptions apply only where legal conditions are met and disclosed. Truly personalised goods may be treated differently; choosing a standard catalogue size is not automatically personalisation. Clothing, footwear, discounted goods and opened packaging are not subject to a blanket exclusion. Conformity claims are governed by statutory guarantee rights independently of the withdrawal period.
