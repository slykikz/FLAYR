# Movement takes your shape.

There is no single way to begin. Some find their rhythm before the city wakes up. Others find it in a final shot, on their way home, or on a detour with no destination.

FLAYR starts with that freedom. Where performance meets personal expression. With a desire to create pieces that become part of the movement, without defining the person wearing them.

## The city as a starting point

Lisbon is our visual inspiration: hard light on concrete, changing rhythms, streets that never repeat themselves. Our horizon stretches further.

## A collection in the making

The Movement Collection is a concept. These images explore a creative direction; the products, materials and features in this demonstration are not yet a commercial offer.

The next chapter takes care: design, prototypes, testing and informed choices.

**Move beyond limits.**
